- **An unfinished status mark is a ring.** Since statement headings stopped
  repeating status words (#839), their mark was a dot for every state but the
  finished ones, and proved, defined and statement formalized share nearly the
  same green. The states still to state or prove (ready to prove, statement
  formalized, ready to state, planned) now take a ring, as the explorer's
  swatches leave them unfilled, so an unproved statement no longer looks proved.
