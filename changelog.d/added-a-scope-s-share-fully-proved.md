  status of its least complete statement, but its search hit and its row under
  Browse now show beside that status how much of it is fully proved, such as
  "74% fully proved", with the counts on hover, and the map gives each part's
  share in its name, its hover text and the reading pane. The share counts and
  rounds as the home page's "Fully proved" figure does, so the whole roadmap's
  is that figure. Scope entries in `dependencies/index.json` and in the scope
  payloads carry the counts as `statements` and `fully_proved`, and the map's
  part summaries count `fully_proved` beside `results` (#856).
